## The idea

Picture a farmer in Vidarbha who wants to plant a new orchard. He opens his phone and sees his plot: its ID, its map from the drone survey, and a green mark that says "title guaranteed". At the bank, the manager checks the same record in seconds and approves a loan against the land the same afternoon.

Picture a family in Lucknow buying their first flat. The record shows the owner, every loan on the property and any linked court case, all in one place. They buy with confidence, and the record moves to their name the moment the sale is registered.

Picture a district judge who opens a land case and finds the full file, every page scanned and linked, ready on her screen.

I believe certainty of ownership is one of the quiet foundations of a rich country. Land is the largest store of wealth most of our families have. When ownership is certain, land becomes capital: you can sell it, borrow against it and build on it with confidence. And when the law itself is one clean, searchable record, every citizen and every business knows exactly where they stand.

We have already done most of the hard work. The scanning is nearly complete. What remains is to join it up and put the guarantee of the state behind every title.

## Why now

The groundwork is done. **99.81%** of our land records are already computerised, and 37 crore plots carry a unique 14-digit ID.

The prize is investment. Land conflicts hold back about **₹14 lakh crore** of investment, and clear titles set it free.

Clear titles also free our courts. About **66%** of civil cases relate to land or property.

And guaranteed titles open credit. If just 5% of our plots raise an average loan of ₹2 lakh, that is about **₹3.7 lakh crore** of new secured credit (estimate) for farmers, families and small firms.

## The big moves

Here is one clear path, step by step. The details belong to the mission head, the states and the firms who will do the work.

1.  **First, give the job to one accountable leader.** A National Land and Law Records Mission, led by a tech czar of cabinet-minister rank with published targets, reports directly to the Prime Minister ([Chapter 12](/the-third-freedom/a-lean-state-that-delivers)). Land records are a state subject, so the mission works first with states that opt in, and then through one national law once land titles move to the Concurrent List ([Chapter 28](/the-third-freedom/locking-in-the-third-freedom)).
    
2.  **Next, finish and join every record in 30 days.** Every remaining paper land, registration and revenue file is scanned and turned into structured data: owner, plot ID, area, any loan or claim on the land, and the linked court case. In the same 30 days, every central and state law, rule and notification goes online as searchable text.
    
3.  **Then, bring the courts' records along within a year.** Files of pending cases come first, ready for the AI courts ([Chapter 6](/the-third-freedom/justice-on-time)), and older records follow. The Supreme Court's e-Committee and each High Court set the deadlines, paid from the existing e-Courts budget brought forward.
    
4.  **Throughout, let any certified firm do the work.** The mission publishes the record specification, a certification test and one fixed price per record, set from independent cost estimates. Any certified firm, large or small, takes work in any district and is paid once its records pass checks and a random audit, just as Aadhaar was built.
    
5.  **Finally, turn records into guaranteed titles.** A Land Titling Act, based on NITI Aayog's 2020 model law, opens a public objection window with an SMS to every name on the record, and every customary or Forest Rights claim is heard in the same window. When the window closes, the title is final. Any claim filed in time is decided within 90 days, with one appeal in 30, and then that title is final too. After that, no title is ever reopened: if anyone later proves a valid claim, a Title Guarantee Fund pays them market value, and the titleholder keeps the land.
    

## Today vs the new way

Here is how land and legal records compare for owners, buyers and courts.

| Outcome | Today | The new way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Records, maps and registration joined up | 84.32% of villages | Every village, joined to its plot ID and map |
| Ownership updated on sale | Automatically in 17 states | Automatically in every state |
| Strength of a land title | Presumptive, open to challenge | Final and guaranteed by the state after one window; a later valid claim is paid market value |
| Court records digitised | 660 crore of about 3,100 crore pages | All of them within 1 year, pending cases first |
| Laws in one searchable place | Spread across many sources | Every central and state law, rule and notification, in 30 days |

| How the guarantee is funded | Stamp duty and registration fees on every sale | 0.5% of property tax, about ₹1,900 crore a year (estimate); stamp duty abolished ([Chapter 1](/the-third-freedom/tax-keep-what-you-earn)) |

The new way turns records we already hold into titles people can trust, lend against and build on.

## Proof it works

**Guaranteed titles have worked for over 160 years.** Australia's Torrens system, in use since 1858, makes the register conclusive and pays anyone who loses land through a registry error from an assurance fund. New Zealand and parts of Canada run the same model.

**Rwanda titled a whole country in five years.** Its Land Tenure Regularisation programme registered about 10.3 million parcels between 2009 and 2013, at about US$6 a parcel.

**We have done the hard parts at home.** Karnataka's Bhoomi computerised land records in the early 2000s and became the national model. SVAMITVA has issued 2.35 crore drone-mapped property cards, and Rajasthan passed India's first title-certification law in 2016. Aadhaar enrolled 119 crore people through certified agencies paid per verified record.

## What success looks like

-   **In the first 30 days:** every land and revenue record is scanned and structured, and every law is online as searchable text.
-   **Within 1 year:** every pending court case file is digital by month 6, and every legacy court record by month 12.
-   **Within 1 year:** every record is joined to its plot ID and map, and registration updates ownership automatically in every state.
-   **Within 1 year:** at least 20 states have adopted the Land Titling Act and the first guaranteed titles are issued.
-   **After year 1:** every claim filed in an objection window is decided within 90 days, with one appeal in 30.

## Guardrail ideas

*Ideas to start the conversation, for the builders, experts and the public to shape together.*

-   **Final means final.** Once the window and any claim on the land are decided, the title cannot be reopened by any court or office; every later claim is settled only in money, at market value, within 180 days.
-   **Owners see every change.** Every edit is logged with the officer's ID, and the owner receives an SMS within 1 hour of any change to the record.
-   **Personal details stay private.** Public view shows only the plot ID, area and owner initials, with full data shown to the owner, to banks with the owner's consent, and to courts.
-   **Every heir and every vulnerable owner is protected.** Each legal heir is notified of any inheritance change, and minors or people lacking capacity keep their right to compensation until 3 years after the incapacity ends.
-   **Quality earns full pay.** Firms are paid in full at 99.5% field accuracy or more, fix any errors at their own cost, and lose certification after repeated low scores.

## FAQs

**Q:** What if someone misses the objection window?

**A:** The true owner is fully protected. Anyone later proven in court to be the true owner receives market value from the Title Guarantee Fund within 180 days, and the fund recovers the money from the fraudster and any official who altered the record.

**Q:** Will forest dwellers and customary owners be overlooked?

**A:** Every claim counts, under one rule for all, and I think this is the most important question to ask. During the window, every forest village holds a gram sabha meeting where families file customary and Forest Rights Act claims together, with help on the spot, and anyone can also file at any tehsil or online. Each claim is decided within 90 days, with one appeal in 30. Then the title is final and the matter is closed for good. If a valid claim surfaces later, it is never left hanging: the claimant is paid market value from the Title Guarantee Fund, quickly and in full.

**Q:** Can court records really be done in a year?

**A:** Yes, by starting with what matters most. Pending case files come first, by month 6, so the courts benefit right away. Our IT and business-process industry can field the roughly 74,000 operators needed, and the work is paid from the existing e-Courts budget.
