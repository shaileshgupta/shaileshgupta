**That India's future is a choice we make.** Everything in this book rests on decisions that can be made now, with tools India already has and ideas already proven. It calls for courage and clarity, and the path is open today.

**That bold and disciplined belong together.** Every idea here comes with its cost, its timeline, its legal route and its guardrails. Ambition and discipline make each other stronger.

**That the talent is already here.** India's future will be built by its own people: its young people, entrepreneurs, engineers, farmers and teachers, once the field is level and open.

**That political will is the scarcest resource.** Ideas are plentiful and money can be raised. The will to act on everything at once, in one term, is rare and precious. Citizens who know the numbers are what make it possible.

**That this playbook belongs to every country on the path to development.** If one country can leap from developing to developed in one generation, any country can. I hope India leads the way.
