Think of a vegetable seller who takes a UPI payment, a farmer who checks his land record on a phone, a shop owner whose GST invoice reaches her buyer in seconds. Every great economy runs on trusted rails like these: money that moves instantly, taxes that settle themselves, banks that reach everyone, records that everyone believes, and courts that decide on time. India has already built the hardest parts. UPI carries almost every rupee, Aadhaar identifies more than a billion people, and GST links businesses across the country. This Part finishes the job.

I group these six chapters because each one makes the next possible. Digital money makes a simple bank transaction tax possible. Clean digital land records make a fair property tax possible. Open, secure public infrastructure lets fintech banks serve every village. Digital court records let AI-assisted courts decide every case in months, so every contract, loan and title can be enforced quickly. With these rails in place, money comes into the open, honest work is rewarded, and the state can see where every rupee goes. Every reform in the rest of this book runs on them, which is why they come first.
