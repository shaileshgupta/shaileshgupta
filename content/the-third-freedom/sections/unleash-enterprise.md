I think India's greatest resource is the energy of its people. Walk down any market street and you will meet someone ready to start a shop, open a factory, build a home or farm at scale. This Part gives them the open field to do it.

These reforms sit together because they cost almost nothing and pay back fastest. Most of them clear away rules rather than add spending. One business ID and registration in place of licences make starting simple. An AI review of every law clears the backlog of outdated rules in one Great Repeal Act. Freer cities create more homes near the jobs. Welcoming capital at scale, while keeping Indian control of what is built here, brings investment. Free sale and leasing of farmland lets farmers earn more and lets agriculture grow. Together they unlock the quickest growth in the book, and the early wins build the trust and revenue that the larger reforms need.
