## The idea

Picture an honest district engineer in Odisha. She files nothing. Once a year the system builds her net-worth statement from land, bank, share and vehicle records, and she checks it on her phone in five minutes. Her record speaks for her, and her career rises on her work.

Picture a citizen in Chennai opening the Election Commission portal and seeing, within two days, who gave money to every party and how much.

Picture a factory owner in Ludhiana whose inspection is decided by an officer in another state, a stranger to him, on recorded evidence and geo-tagged photos. The inspection is about the factory, and only the factory.

I believe most public servants are honest, and they deserve a system that proves it. We have tried to fight corruption one bribe at a time, through long cases and paper declarations. Hong Kong chose a simpler path in 1971: ask officials to explain wealth beyond their pay. It worked, and I think it can work for us.

When wealth is visible, when political money is public, and when audits are faceless, honesty becomes easy and the rare dishonest act becomes easy to see. All the data is already on file; we only need to join it.

## Why now

Here is why I think we can move now.

We have room to rise. India scores **39 out of 100** on Transparency International's index, and every point up means cleaner services and more trust.

Our courts already convict well. The CBI's conviction rate was **71.7%**, so what we need is speed, and a simpler test of wealth gives us that.

Voters have asked to know who funds politics. The Supreme Court struck down anonymous electoral bonds, which had raised **₹16,518 crore**, in February 2024, opening the way for fully open funding.

The payoff is large. A register costing about **₹650 crore** to build could save about ₹30,000 crore a year in government buying (estimate).

## The big moves

Here is the path I see, one step after another.

1.  **First, build one automatic, public wealth register.** Every government employee at every level, from a minister to a clerk, is covered, and so is every person and firm registered with the government in a contractual role: every contractor, supplier, consultant and contract worker, and for a firm, its owners, directors and ultimate beneficial owners, the real people behind every layer of companies and trusts. The system compiles each person's yearly statement from land, bank, share, vehicle, gold and company records, the person simply checks and corrects it, and a summary is public for anyone to see.
    
2.  **Next, offer a clean start.** The 30-day amnesty in [Chapter 1](/the-third-freedom/tax-keep-what-you-earn) runs first, so everyone can set their record straight before any test begins.
    
3.  **Then ask for an explanation where wealth runs far ahead of income.** For example, a notice could issue only where wealth exceeds all recorded lawful income by a wide margin, approved by a Lokpal or Lokayukta bench. The person has 30 days to explain, and a special court decides; wealth left unexplained returns to the public.
    
4.  **At the same time, open every political rupee to view.** Parties receive money through digital, verified "open bonds", with donor, amount and party published within 48 hours, and all campaign spending is digital and logged.
    
5.  **Finally, make audits faceless, with one owner for every date.** An algorithm assigns every inquiry, audit and inspection to a random officer in another state, with all contact recorded, as faceless tax assessment already does. A Chief Integrity Officer reporting to the Prime Minister ([Chapter 12](/the-third-freedom/a-lean-state-that-delivers)) owns every deadline.
    

## Today vs the new way

Here is how I see the new way comparing with today on visibility, speed and value.

| Outcome | Today | The new way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Declaring assets | Candidates file affidavits; most officials and contractors file on paper or not at all | One automatic yearly statement for every government employee and every registered contractor, with a public summary; nothing to file |
| Proving corruption | Each bribe proven case by case; 7,229 CBI cases await trial | Unexplained wealth explained in 30 days, decided by a court in 180 days |
| Political donations | ₹3,811 crore through electoral trusts in 2024-25 | Every donor, amount and party public within 48 hours |
| Audits and inspections | Assigned through regular channels | Assigned at random to an officer in another state, fully recorded |
| Cost to build | Data already held across registries | About ₹650 crore, plus about ₹150 crore a year to run (estimate) |

For a small cost, every rupee of public wealth and political money becomes visible, and public buying gets better value.

## Proof it works

**Hong Kong built a clean city on one rule.** Since 1971, its Prevention of Bribery Ordinance has asked any official whose living standard or assets exceed official pay to give a satisfactory explanation. It underpins the record of its anti-corruption commission.

**The United Kingdom asks people to explain their wealth.** Unexplained wealth orders under the Criminal Finances Act 2017 require a person to show how they paid for property. The first order, against Zamira Hajiyeva, was upheld, and the UK Supreme Court refused her appeal in 2020.

**India already runs faceless reviews.** From 2019 the income-tax department allotted cases at random to officers in other cities, starting with 58,319 cases, with all contact through a portal.

**Ukraine opened every official's wealth to the public.** In 2016 it launched online asset declarations that anyone can search, and the first round brought the declared wealth of its top officials into public view at once. The United Kingdom has published the real owners of every company, its "people with significant control", on a free public register since 2016. India itself has asked every election candidate to publish an affidavit of assets since 2003, so voters already read these records.

**Open party funding works.** The UK Electoral Commission publishes every reportable donation to every party, by name, each quarter.

## What success looks like

-   **In the first 30 days:** draft statements are compiled for every central minister, MP and senior officer, the Election Funding Act is passed, and 48-hour disclosure starts for all new donations.
-   **In the first 30 days:** all new central vigilance inquiries go to random officers in other states, and the Chief Integrity Officer is appointed.
-   **Within 1 year:** the Illicit Enrichment and Forfeiture Act is passed, and 100 special-court benches are working on a 180-day clock.
-   **Within 1 year:** every government employee and every registered contractor has a public wealth summary, the first full match against bank and property records is complete, and faceless review extends to building permits, GST audits and factory inspections.
-   **After year 1:** government buying saves about ₹30,000 crore a year (estimate).

## Guardrail ideas

*Ideas to start the conversation, for the builders, experts and the public to shape together.*

-   **Every decision rests with a court.** Wealth is returned only by court order, with free legal aid and a High Court appeal decided within 90 days.
-   **Honest people get a simple fix.** Small gaps below 25% and ₹25 lakh receive an automatic "correct your data" request instead of a case.
-   **Fair to every party.** Cases are picked by risk score plus a 10% purely random sample, with notice counts by state and party published every month.
-   **Public summary, private details.** The public summary shows totals by asset class and the yearly change, never account numbers, addresses or a child's details. Full records are open only to the Lokpal or Lokayukta and the courts, every access is logged and shown to the person, and misuse of the data is a criminal offence.
-   **Every contract carries the same duty.** Registering as a government contractor, supplier or consultant includes consent to the yearly statement for the firm's owners, directors and ultimate beneficial owners, traced through every holding company and trust. The real owners of every contracting firm are public by name, and a firm whose real owners are left out is paid nothing until the record is complete.
-   **Every officer understands every file.** Every document is machine-translated into English and Hindi within 24 hours with the original attached, and any disputed translation is checked by a certified translator within 7 days.

## FAQs

**Q:** Does this treat honest officials as suspects?

**A:** It gives honest officials a public record that proves their integrity. Lawful income counts everything on record, including a spouse's income, inheritance and gifts. A notice needs a gap of more than 25% and ₹25 lakh, a 30-day chance to explain, and a court order. The duty to account for assets beyond known income has been upheld by the Supreme Court (*Wasudeo Kaidalwar*, 1981).

**Q:** Isn't a public wealth list an invasion of privacy?

**A:** It protects honest people by making their record speak for them. Anyone paid from public money accepts a public summary, as every election candidate has done since 2003. The public sees totals and yearly changes only; account numbers, addresses and family details stay private, open only to the Lokpal and the courts, with every access logged.

**Q:** Could it be used against the opposition?

**A:** It is built to be fair to all. Cases are chosen by risk score and a random sample, a Lokpal or Lokayukta bench approves every notice, and counts by party are published every month for everyone to see.

**Q:** How is income measured once income tax ends ([Chapter 1](/the-third-freedom/tax-keep-what-you-earn))?

**A:** More precisely than before. The bank-transaction ledger records every credit and its source, and the last 10 years of income-tax returns stay archived for this test.
