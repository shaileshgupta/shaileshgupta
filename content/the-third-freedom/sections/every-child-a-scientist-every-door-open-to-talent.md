## The idea

Meet Priya. She is in class 4 at a village school in Bihar. Every morning she spends an hour on maths. A tablet in her classroom carries a tutor that speaks Maithili, knows exactly where she is stuck and gives her the next small step. It works offline, all day. Her teacher holds a ready lesson plan for each day, and a maths specialist visits the cluster every week.

Across the state, a twelve-year-old boy in a madrasa spends his morning on the same core: equations, a science experiment, his first lines of code. In the afternoon and at weekends he studies the Quran with the teachers his family trusts. A girl in a gurukul and a child in a Vedic pathshala sit the same national tests and earn certificates that every employer and university recognises. Faith and science sit comfortably together.

Ten years later Priya picks a university by looking up its audited results: how many graduates found jobs and what they earn a year later. She chooses well, and her loan follows her. In her lab she works beside a researcher from abroad who arrived on a 10-year visa, granted in 30 days.

I believe talent is spread evenly across India, and opportunity can be too. Daily practice, good tools, clear results and open doors turn every child's curiosity into skill. Singapore had no oil and no hinterland, so it bet everything on its people. We have 24.7 crore schoolchildren. That is our greatest resource, and this chapter is how we put it to work.

## Why now

The biggest gain is in the early years. Today about **1 in 3** rural class 3 children can do a subtraction. Every point we add here compounds for a lifetime.

That start can reach every school. The Right to Education Act leaves madrasas, Vedic pathshalas and schools mainly giving religious instruction outside its core (Section 1(5)), and Uttar Pradesh alone has about **17 lakh** pupils in recognised madrasas. In November 2024 the Supreme Court confirmed that the state may set standards in these schools, while their Article 30 rights stay whole.

The cost of a personal coach has fallen to almost nothing. A tutor at about **₹100** per child a year (estimate) can sit beside every child in the country.

Our young people are already voting with their feet. More than **18 lakh** Indians study abroad. Great universities at home can keep much of that talent and money here.

And the world is ready to visit. We welcomed **99.5 lakh** foreign tourists in 2024, while Vietnam welcomed 17.6 million. Easy entry can lift India to its natural place.

## The big moves

Here is the path I see, one step after another.

1.  **First, give every child a daily hour of maths.** We begin with the timetable: an hour of maths every day, with reading in the mother tongue, and science and coding added from class 3. Teachers get ready daily lessons from NCERT, online training and a maths specialist for each cluster, so every lesson is ready to teach.
    
2.  **Next, put a tutor beside every child.** We build an AI tutor in all 22 scheduled languages on an open standard, like UPI, so any firm that passes the certification test can serve any school. It runs offline on school tablets and adapts to each child, free as part of schooling.
    
3.  **Then, measure learning gain and lift every school.** Every child in classes 3, 5 and 8 takes a national test, and each school is scored on how far its children grew. This lets a school in a poor area shine. Schools with the lowest gains get a 90-day plan and, if needed, a proven partner to run them, with fees at zero and teachers in place.
    
4.  **Alongside, one national core in every school of every faith.** A National School Standards Act, under the Concurrent List entry on education, asks every institution that is a child's main school, from government and private schools to madrasas, pathshalas, gurukuls, convents and seminaries, to teach this daily core and sit the national tests. Deleting Section 1(5) of the RTE Act gives every child aged 6 to 14 the same right to the same education, and every child earns a board- or UGC-recognised certificate that carries full value.
    
5.  **Faith teaching flourishes beside it, with a smooth one-year move.** Any school teaches religion, scripture or traditional knowledge outside core hours and at weekends, and minority schools keep full control of admissions, management, staff, language and faith teaching. Each institution adopts the core within a year or becomes a supplementary school whose pupils join a nearby school for the day, faith-school teachers can qualify through a short certified course, and the AI tutor reaches every school.
    
6.  **After school, open universities and let results speak.** Any institution can register online by filing a disclosure, foreign campuses included. Each one publishes audited job outcomes, checked against EPFO and bank data ([Chapter 1](/the-third-freedom/tax-keep-what-you-earn)), and student loans follow the best results.
    
7.  **Finally, open our doors to talent and visitors.** A 10-year Talent Visa, decided within 30 days, welcomes top scientists, graduates and founders, and citizens of 50 low-risk countries can visit visa-free for up to 90 days. They bring skills, jobs and foreign exchange.

## Proof it works

**India can test every child.** The PARAKH national assessment tested about 21 lakh students in 2024 and published results for every state. The tool for measuring learning at scale is already proven.

**A Brazilian state rewarded learning.** Since 2007, Ceará has shared part of its tax revenue with municipalities according to their pupils' learning. Its city of Sobral rose to the top of Brazil's national primary-school rankings.

**One core already reaches faith schools.** In 2018 Uttar Pradesh introduced NCERT textbooks in board madrasas, making maths and science part of the madrasa day, and in 2024 the Supreme Court confirmed the state's power to set standards in madrasas while protecting their right to exist. England holds every full-time independent school, faith schools included, to standards that require mathematical and scientific education.

**An AI tutor lifted scores in Nigeria.** In a 2024 World Bank pilot in Edo State, six weeks of after-school tutoring with an AI assistant raised students' English scores well beyond the comparison group.

**Open doors bring visitors and talent.** Thailand opened 60-day visa-free entry to citizens of 93 countries in July 2024. The UAE has offered 10-year Golden Visas to investors, scientists and exceptional talent since 2019.

## What success looks like

-   **In the first 30 days:** the daily STEM timetable is notified, the National School Standards Bill and the RTE amendment are introduced, NCERT lessons are released, the university disclosure portal is live and the Talent Visa is open.
-   **Within 1 year:** the AI tutor is live in all 22 languages, and 1.5 crore tablets are in classrooms.
-   **Within 1 year:** every school that is a child's main school, faith schools included, teaches the full core, or its pupils have a confirmed seat in a nearby school.
-   **Within 1 year:** the first census test results are public by month 7, and the first audited university outcome tables are published.
-   **After year 1:** class 3 maths scores rise by at least 5 points by month 18.
-   **After year 1:** class 3 subtraction reaches 50% within 3 years, and quarterly results by school type show children in faith schools closing the gap.

## Guardrail ideas

*Ideas to start the conversation, for the builders, experts and the public to shape together.*

-   **The tutor is accurate in every language.** Each language version passes a high accuracy bar on an NCERT question bank before launch, with quarterly audits and human review of flagged answers.
-   **Children's data stays with children.** The tutor is fully ad-free, with children's data used only for learning, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
-   **Results are trusted.** Papers are randomised on tablets with external invigilators, and university outcomes are checked against EPFO and bank data with random audits.
-   **Every faith is treated alike.** The inspection list and results are published every quarter by school type and faith, so even-handed treatment is visible to all.
-   **A confirmed seat comes first.** Pupils move only once a seat is confirmed within 1 km for classes 1 to 5 and 3 km for classes 6 to 8, with hostels and meals staying where they are.
-   **Open entry stays safe.** Every Talent Visa comes with a 30-day background check and biometrics, and a quick review of any country whose overstay rate rises above 1%.

## FAQs

**Q:** Can teachers really teach daily coding and maths?

**A:** It is a fair worry, and we answer it with full support for every teacher. Scripted daily lessons, an AI co-teacher in the classroom, 50 hours of training and a maths specialist in every cluster make each lesson ready to teach.

**Q:** How does the tutor reach the third of schools that are still getting internet?

**A:** The tutor reaches them fully. It runs offline on the tablet and syncs once a week, and BharatNet and satellite links reach these schools first.

**Q:** Does one core weaken minority rights under Articles 25 and 30?

**A:** I understand the concern, and minority rights stay whole. Every school stays open, and minority schools keep full control of admissions, management, staff, language and faith teaching. The state sets only academic standards, which *T.M.A. Pai* and the 2024 judgment allow, and it covers madrasas, pathshalas, gurukuls, convents and seminaries alike.

**Q:** Will talent visas take Indian jobs?

**A:** They create jobs for Indians. A salary floor of ₹30 lakh a year means the visa fills senior roles that help firms grow and hire, and founders of funded startups build new companies here.
