## The idea

Picture a widow in Jharkhand on the 1st of the month. ₹2,000 lands in her own bank account, on time, every month. She buys rice, dal and oil at any ration shop or market she chooses, with full dignity and full choice.

Picture her nephew, 24 and out of work. The same transfer carries him for a year while he trains as an electrician. As his wages rise, the transfer steps down gently, and every extra rupee he earns leaves him better off. Within a year he stands on his own feet.

Picture a young woman sitting a central recruitment test. Her name, surname and photo are hidden. She is judged on her answers alone, and she gets the job. If she ever meets bias at work or in a bank, a commission hears her and decides within 30 days.

I believe help works best when it goes to people by need, in cash, straight to them, and lifts them towards work. Every citizen deserves an equal chance, judged on merit, with bias fought case by case and fast. One rule for every citizen, applied with care, is both fair and kind.

## Why now

The money is ready to work far harder. We spend about **₹4.30 lakh crore** a year on major subsidies for food, fertiliser and fuel. One well-aimed transfer can do more for the poor at well under half of that.

We already know cash works. Direct Benefit Transfer saved about **₹3.48 lakh crore** in leakages from 2009 to 2024.

Our grain stores are already full. Central rice stocks were about **four times** the buffer norm in early 2026, so grain can move to market while buffers stay full.

And talent is waiting at the top. SC and ST officers hold as little as **4.8%** of some top posts, against a 22.5% quota. Blind selection, open data and fast bias rulings can open those doors on merit.

## The big moves

Here is the path I suggest, one step at a time.

1.  **First, switch on one simple test and one payment.** In the first 30 days an eligibility engine checks every household against one income test, the same for every citizen, and tells each family by SMS. Payments start on the 1st of the month, for example ₹2,000 per household, straight into an Aadhaar-linked account ([Chapter 2](/the-third-freedom/every-rupee-digital)).
    
2.  **Next, step subsidies down as the cash steps in.** Food, fertiliser, LPG and kerosene subsidies come down in four quarterly steps, so families adjust gradually. Grain stores stay full, the surplus is sold openly, and ration shops keep selling at cost, so the cash buys grain anywhere.
    
3.  **Then, walk alongside every family towards work.** Families who cannot work keep the full amount. For everyone else, the transfer eases down over a year while an adult trains, every rupee earned leaves them better off, and anyone still looking gets a paid Work Offer, urban or rural.
    
4.  **Within the year, give every citizen the same fair chance.** One Equal Opportunity Act makes merit the test for new central jobs and seats, with names and photos hidden in written tests and reasonable accommodation for every disabled candidate. Help is offered by need, so a family on the transfer can afford coaching.
    
5.  **All along, fight bias case by case.** An Equal Opportunity Commission hears any citizen's complaint and decides within 30 days, and every central recruiter publishes who applied and who was hired, so any gap shows up and gets looked at.
    

## Today vs the new way

Here is how one transfer and one equal-opportunity law compare with today.

| Outcome | Today | The new way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central spending on major subsidies | About ₹4.30 lakh crore a year | About ₹1.70 lakh crore in year 1, about ₹0.94 lakh crore after (estimate) |
| Money freed for other uses | Nil | About ₹2.60 lakh crore in year 1, rising to about ₹3.35 lakh crore a year (estimate) |
| Support for a poor household | 25 kg of free grain a month for a five-person household | ₹2,000 a month, enough for about 45 kg of rice at retail (estimate) |
| How help reaches people | Goods priced below cost through long supply chains | Cash on the 1st of each month into the household's own account |
| Grain stocks | Rice about four times the buffer norm | At or above the norm, with the surplus sold by open e-auction |
| Work for those still looking | A rural employment guarantee | One Work Offer for every citizen, urban or rural |
| Fair chance at senior posts | SC and ST officers as little as 4.8% of some top posts | Blind selection, published hiring data, bias rulings within 30 days |

Poor families get more choice and a path to work, the treasury frees well over ₹2 lakh crore a year, and every citizen competes on the same fair terms.

## Proof it works

**India's own cash rails already deliver.** Moving benefits to direct bank transfers saved about ₹3.48 lakh crore in leakages from 2009 to 2024. The Self-Reliance Transfer runs on the same proven rails.

**Brazil folded many benefits into one.** Since 2003, Bolsa Família has replaced several separate benefits with one means-tested monthly cash transfer. It reached 18.84 million families in February 2026.

**America tied welfare to work.** Its 1996 welfare reform linked federal cash aid to work and set a lifetime limit of 60 months, putting work at the centre of support.

**A screen opened the door to talent.** When American orchestras auditioned players behind a screen, women became far more likely to advance past the early rounds. Judging the performance alone found the best players.

## What success looks like

-   **In the first 30 days:** the eligibility engine is live, every household receives an SMS saying "eligible", "not eligible" or "appeal", the first transfers are paid, and the Equal Opportunity Commission takes complaints online.
-   **Within 1 year:** one cash transfer has fully replaced central subsidies, every can-work recipient is in training, and the Work Offer is live in every district, urban and rural.
-   **Within 1 year:** merit is the test for new central recruitment and admissions, with blind selection everywhere, and a quarterly public dashboard shows recipients, exits to jobs, wages and child nutrition.
-   **After year 1:** the transfer's cost settles at about **₹0.94 lakh crore** a year, and the saving rises to about **₹3.35 lakh crore** a year (estimate).

## Guardrail ideas

*Ideas to start the conversation, for the builders, experts and the public to shape together.*

-   **Every child stays well fed.** A hunger trigger: if food inflation stays above 8% for 2 months, or child wasting rises in any district, the next step pauses, with a ₹500 monthly top-up if it fires in the final quarter, and buffer grain goes to auction within 7 days.
-   **Every eligible family is included.** Wrongful rejection stays under 1%, checked quarterly, with appeals decided in 7 days and an in-person check within 48 hours when biometrics fail.
-   **Bias complaints are settled on time.** The Commission pays the complainant ₹10,000 for any case it takes longer than 30 days to decide.
-   **Grain stores stay full.** The Food Corporation chairman reports to Parliament within 15 days whenever stock dips below the norm on a quarter date.

## FAQs

**Q:** Will families eat as well without free grain?

**A:** Families gain more food and more choice. ₹2,000 buys about 45 kg of rice at retail (estimate), more than the 25 kg a five-person household receives today. Households who cannot work keep the full amount, and the hunger trigger pauses the change if prices or child nutrition move the wrong way.

**Q:** Will the courts uphold merit as the one test?

**A:** The plan rests on firm legal ground. Articles 15 and 16 enable quotas and leave the choice to Parliament, and the Supreme Court has held that reservation in promotions is a policy choice rather than a fundamental right (2020). Article 335 asks that SC and ST claims be taken into account, which published representation data and bias audits provide.

**Q:** What if more households qualify than the budget allows?

**A:** Every eligible household is paid, on one fair rule. The income line adjusts by at most 10% for everyone, published in month 1, and any remaining cost is met from the subsidy saving and reported to Parliament within 30 days.
