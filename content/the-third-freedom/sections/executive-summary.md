Imagine a young Indian with an idea on a Monday morning. By evening she has registered her business, opened a bank account on her phone and made her first sale. I believe India can become a country where that is ordinary, and move from developing to developed within one generation, simply by freeing its people to build. The talent is here, the demographic window is open, and the key ideas are already proven in India or abroad. This book sets out 28 reforms that turn that promise into a clear path. It starts with the money, tax and digital rails that everything else runs on, then orders the rest by return on effort, so the fastest and cheapest wins come early. Every action starts in the first 30 days or is complete within 1 year, guided by a few simple principles: one country, one law; minimum government, maximum governance; register, don't ask permission; fair published prices; the Aadhaar way of rolling out; sovereignty first; pay for outcomes; no cap on dreams; Europe for quality of life and America for capitalism; and a guardrail for every rule.

## What it adds up to

Put the pieces together and here is the country you get:

-   A tax system that settles itself, with almost no forms.
-   A state that buys fast at a fair price and pays for results.
-   Courts and police that work to deadlines.
-   A market where any young Indian can start, hire, raise money and sell anywhere.
-   Indian companies free to grow as large as Google or Apple and lead the world, with the state policing conduct.

The money comes from simpler taxes, well-targeted support in place of leaky subsidies, and the sale of assets that thrive better in private hands. Published data, deadlines and clear consequences guard every step. Each chapter gives the direction and the first steps; the finer details are for the people who will build it, and I hope you are one of them.
