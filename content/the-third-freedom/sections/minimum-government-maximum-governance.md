Picture a citizen who visits a government office and leaves an hour later with the job done, or never needs to visit at all. A great country needs a great state: one that does a few things superbly and earns the trust of every citizen. This Part reshapes government to deliver exactly that.

These three chapters work as one. A lean state, staffed by skilled people on market pay and performance contracts, can focus on what only a state can do. Open wealth records and public political funding make every rupee traceable. A public scorecard for every chief minister, mayor and district head lets the best ideas spread across the country. The reforms largely fund themselves through asset sales and savings, and they unlock something priceless: a government that citizens can see working for them.
