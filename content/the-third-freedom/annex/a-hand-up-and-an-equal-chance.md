## The plan

**One rule for welfare: the Self-Reliance Transfer (SRT)**

1.  **All central subsidies end** (food, fertiliser, LPG, kerosene) in 4 quarterly steps of 25%. One cash transfer replaces them. This is not a subsidy under another name: a subsidy lowers the price of a good for everyone who buys it, while the transfer is income support for a household in need, on one test and with a taper. What stays free to the citizen is a short list of rights and public goods: schooling to age 14, legal aid in court, ID and rollout services, and public-health measures such as contraception and the learning library (Chapters 6, 15 and 25, and the Aadhaar playbook).
2.  **One test for every citizen.** A household qualifies if its income is under ₹1.2 lakh a year and its net assets are under ₹10 lakh, not counting one home under 60 square metres. There is no carve-out by caste, religion, occupation or region. Farmers qualify on the same test as everyone else, with no separate per-acre payment. Data comes from bank inflows ([Chapter 1](/the-third-freedom/tax-keep-what-you-earn)), land records ([Chapter 5](/the-third-freedom/land-you-truly-own)) and the vehicle register.
3.  **Amount:** ₹2,000 a month per household, paid by DBT on the 1st of each month into an Aadhaar-linked account ([Chapter 2](/the-third-freedom/every-rupee-digital)).
4.  **Two tiers, set per household:**
    -   *Cannot work:* every adult in the household is aged 70 or more, has a disability of 40% or more on a UDID card (the national disability ID), or is the one named carer of a child under 3 or a disabled dependant (one carer per household). No taper; checked again each year.
    -   *Can work:* every other household, including able adults aged 61 to 69.
5.  **Fixed 12-month taper for the can-work tier:**

| Months | Share of full transfer (%) | Transfer (₹ a month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | 100 | 2,000 |
| 4 to 6 | 75 | 1,500 |
| 7 to 9 | 50 | 1,000 |
| 10 to 12 | 25 | 500 |
| From month 13 | 0 | zero |

-   Months 1 to 3: Within 30 days the adult joins skill training or an apprenticeship of at least 20 hours a week from the Skill India or apprenticeship scheme list, or the transfer pauses until they enrol.
-   No cliff: each ₹3 earned cuts the transfer by ₹1, so working always pays more than not working.
-   Re-entry is allowed after 12 months off the transfer, up to a lifetime total of 48 months in the can-work tier.

6.  **The Work Offer.** VB-G RAM G, the rural employment guarantee that replaced MGNREGA on 1 July 2026, becomes one Work Offer for every citizen, urban or rural, on the same terms. Anyone who finishes training without a job is offered paid public work (clean-ups of air, rivers and dumpsites, construction) at the state minimum wage, with wages paid within 15 days. Turning it down ends the transfer.
7.  **Budget lock.** The ₹1.70 lakh crore budget covers about 9 crore households (arithmetic below). If the eligibility engine finds more than 9 crore in month 1, the income line is lowered in the same proportion for everyone, by at most 10% (never below ₹1.08 lakh), and published in month 1. Any cost still above ₹1.70 lakh crore is met first from the ₹10,000 crore transfer reserve and then from the net subsidy saving, and reported to Parliament within 30 days.
8.  **Grain buffers stay full.** The Food Corporation of India (FCI) keeps stock at or above the official buffer norm on every quarter date. Stock above the norm is sold by open e-auction. Ration shops stay open and sell at cost, so SRT money buys grain anywhere (One Nation One Ration Card).
9.  **The legal route.** The National Food Security Act is amended so the food entitlement becomes "cash enough to buy the ration", which still meets the duty to feed under Article 21.

**One rule for opportunity: the Equal Opportunity Act**

10.  **Quotas end for new central recruitment and admissions at month 12.** This covers central jobs, central universities and central public-sector companies. Merit is the only test.
     -   The Central Educational Institutions (Reservation in Admission) Act, 2006 and the Central Educational Institutions (Reservation in Teachers' Cadre) Act, 2019 are repealed.
     -   The Department of Personnel withdraws the executive orders that set quotas in central jobs.
     -   Articles 15(4), 15(5), 16(4) and 16(6) enable quotas but do not require them.
11.  **Disability.** The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 is amended. Its 4% job and 5% higher-education reservations become a duty of reasonable accommodation for every candidate who needs it: scribes, extra time and accessible workplaces, enforced by the Commission within 30 days.
12.  **The Equal Opportunity Commission (EOC)** decides any bias complaint (hiring, admission, promotion, housing, lending) within 30 days. Remedies: the decision is reversed, compensation up to ₹25 lakh, and a 3-year ban from government contracts for repeat offenders. The constitutional SC and ST commissions (Articles 338 and 338A) continue until the [Chapter 28](/the-third-freedom/locking-in-the-third-freedom) amendment; the EOC hears every citizen.
13.  **Blind selection.** Names, surnames and photos are hidden in every written test and first-round screening for central jobs and admissions.
14.  **Help is need-based, not birth-based.** Exam fees are one flat amount for every candidate. SRT households pay for coaching from the transfer, or with a skills loan open to every citizen.
15.  **Representation data.** Every central recruiter publishes the make-up of its applicants and hires each year, by group. Any recruiter whose hires from a group fall more than 30% below that group's share of its applicant pool gets a mandatory EOC bias audit. The same rule applies to disabled applicants.

## Cost and return

-   **Households (estimate):** India has about 32 crore households (140 crore people ÷ 4.4 per household). The bottom 20% is about 6.4 crore.
-   **Year-1 SRT cost (estimate):** 6.4 crore × ₹24,000 = ₹1.54 lakh crore, counted as if no household tapers. Adding administration (1%, about ₹1,500 crore) and the cost of carrying the grain buffer (about ₹15,000 crore) gives about ₹1.70 lakh crore.
-   **Capacity check (estimate):** under the taper, a can-work household gets on average 62.5% of the full amount in year 1 (₹15,000). So ₹1.70 lakh crore covers about 9 crore households:

| Tier | Households (crore) | Year-1 amount per household (₹) | Arithmetic | Cost (₹ lakh crore) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cannot work | 2 | 24,000 | 2 crore × ₹24,000 | 0.48 |
| Can work | 7 | 15,000 | 7 crore × ₹15,000 | 1.05 |
| Total, plus administration and buffer | about 9 |  | 0.48 + 1.05, plus administration and buffer | 1.70 |

-   **Net saving in year 1 (estimate):** ₹4.30 − ₹1.70 = about ₹2.60 lakh crore. [Chapter 15](/the-third-freedom/every-child-a-scientist-every-door-open-to-talent) (STEM, ₹24,000 crore) draws on it, and ₹10,000 crore is held as a reserve for the transfer, together ₹34,000 crore. That leaves about ₹2.26 lakh crore in this first year.
-   **Steady state, after year 1 (estimate):** about 2 crore cannot-work households × ₹24,000 = ₹0.48 lakh crore, plus a revolving can-work group of about 1.5 crore households (about ₹0.30 lakh crore), plus the buffer and administration (about ₹0.16 lakh crore). Total about ₹0.94 lakh crore, so the saving rises to about ₹3.35 lakh crore a year. [Chapter 1](/the-third-freedom/tax-keep-what-you-earn)'s tax switch comes at month 12, so its first year counts this steady saving, net of the ₹34,000 crore for STEM and the reserve: about ₹3.01 lakh crore.
-   **Work Offer (estimate):** paid from VB-G RAM G's existing central allocation of ₹95,692 crore (2026-27), with no new money. At ₹400 a day, that pays about 1.9 crore people for 125 days (₹95,692 crore ÷ (125 × ₹400)). If demand exceeds the budget, places go first to SRT households.
-   **Grain sales:** stock above the buffer norm, sold by e-auction, returns cash to FCI. The amount depends on auction prices.
-   **Technology (estimate):** the existing DBT and PFMS payment rails, Aadhaar eKYC, and an eligibility engine that matches bank, land and vehicle data. NIC and NPCI build it in 90 days at under ₹500 crore.
-   **EOC (estimate):** 500 adjudicators (retired judges and officers) at about ₹30 lakh each = ₹150 crore a year. AI triage sorts complaints.

## Timeline

**First 30 days:**

-   Table the NFSA Amendment Bill, the SRT Bill and the Equal Opportunity Bill, together with the repeal of the 2006 and 2019 Central Educational Institutions reservation Acts and the RPwD Act amendment.
-   The eligibility engine goes live. Every household gets an SMS: "eligible", "not eligible" or "appeal".
-   Subsidy step 1 (minus 25%). SRT payments start for every eligible household.
-   The EOC is set up and takes complaints online.

**Within 1 year:**

-   Subsidy steps 2 to 4. All central subsidies reach zero by month 12.
-   Every can-work recipient is enrolled in training. The Work Offer is live in every district, urban and rural.
-   The first can-work cohort completes the 12-month taper, with the Work Offer as the backstop.
-   Quotas end for new central recruitment and admissions at month 12. Blind selection is used everywhere.
-   A quarterly public dashboard shows recipients, exits to jobs, wages and child nutrition.

**Outcomes after year 1:**

-   SRT cost falls to about ₹0.94 lakh crore a year as the can-work tier revolves.
-   Representation data shows whether blind selection and EOC audits close the gaps at senior levels.

## Guardrails

-   **Hunger trigger:** if food inflation (CPI) is above 8% for 2 months, or child wasting in any district rises by 2 points on the Poshan Tracker, the next subsidy step pauses for one quarter and the remaining steps merge, so all subsidies still end by month 12.
    -   The last step (month 10) never pauses. If the trigger fires then, every SRT household gets a ₹500 a month top-up for that quarter, paid from grain-sale proceeds.
    -   Buffer grain is put to e-auction within 7 days. The Finance Ministry owns this.
-   **Exclusion error:** wrongful rejection under 1%, checked by a quarterly audit of 10,000 cases. Appeals are decided in 7 days. If biometrics fail, an in-person check follows within 48 hours.
-   **Fraud:** a false declaration means repaying twice the amount and a 3-year bar from the SRT. 2% of households get random field checks.
-   **Buffer:** stock below the norm on any quarter date means the FCI chairman explains to Parliament within 15 days.
-   **EOC deadline:** every case is decided within 30 days, or the Commission pays the complainant ₹10,000.

## Limits to know

Reserved seats in the Lok Sabha and state assemblies (Articles 330 and 332) run until January 2030 under Article 334. Ending them earlier needs a constitutional amendment with a two-thirds majority and ratification by half the states ([Chapter 28](/the-third-freedom/locking-in-the-third-freedom)). If those votes are not secured, the government brings no extension bill and the seats lapse in January 2030; Sixth Schedule and Article 371 provisions follow the same amendment path. State quota laws, such as Tamil Nadu's 69% in the Ninth Schedule, are state matters: the Centre ends quotas for central posts and seats, and offers states incentive grants to follow.
